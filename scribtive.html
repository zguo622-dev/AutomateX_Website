1 The pack

It starts with 11 documents and a blank form.

A Form G is assembled before it is written. Someone gathers the pack, finds the standards that apply, and carries each fact from the surveys into the certificate.

Then the review comments arrive, and the rounds begin.

Most of this is careful reading. It falls to the people whose judgement the certificate depends on.

2 The paragraph

A fluent paragraph is easy to come by.

Paste the pack into a chat assistant and a paragraph comes back in seconds. It reads well. For a small editorial job, that may be all you need.

A certificate asks more. Whoever signs it needs to know which sentences the pack supports, and which ones only sound right.
