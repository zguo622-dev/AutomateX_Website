11. Summary and next steps

The walkout confirmed that both the P1 and P4 canopies require refurbishment intervention. The P4 structure presents the more complex scope, with fractured glazing, a fractured cast-iron column base, encasement water pooling, blocked box gutters, and general paint degradation.

Scope of the works

Column-encasement pooling. Four columns experience pooling water at the tops of the encasements.

What easy means here.

A deliverable is hard for ordinary reasons. The pack is large, the standards are many, and every statement has to stand up at review.

  1. The whole pack is read.

    The draft is built from all of it, document by document, and not from what the week allowed.

  2. Every statement is evidenced.

    Each one carries its quote and its page, checked against the source it came from.

  3. Gaps surface before review.

    Where the pack cannot support a claim, the draft says so and refers it to your engineer.

  4. Your engineer signs.

    Scribtive drafts. The review, the judgement and the signature stay with your team.

The same team, carrying more deliverables, each one read in more depth.
