How it works

Three steps. No new process.

Scribtive reads what you already have. You review what it produces. The signature stays with you.

Step 01

Load your project docs

Upload what you already have. Scribtive reads structure and content, not page numbers.

  • CIV/003 inspection records
  • RAMS and General Arrangement
  • Evidence surveys and reports
  • Design history and change records
Step 02

Generate the Form G

AI drafts every section against PACE criteria. Gaps are flagged explicitly — never left blank or guessed at.

  • Every claim grounded in source evidence
  • Gaps surfaced, not buried
  • DCC classification applied
  • Consistent output, every run
Step 03

Review and sign off

You review the draft, resolve the gaps, make your engineering calls, and submit. Your expertise, your PI, your signature.

  • Output in your existing format
  • Every claim traceable to source doc
  • You own the deliverable
  • Chartered accountability intact
